Approve, Pause, UndoClickable prototype · scripted agent

Prototype for the HCI project “Oversight Controls for Autonomous AI Agents”. The agent is scripted so every run behaves the same; no real files change. Three conditions: our Console, Yes/No prompts (how today’s tools ask by default) and Plain log (approvals switched off). Defaults follow our survey (n = 12). Keys at a gate: A approve, S skip (Y / N in Yes/No prompts). P pauses.
